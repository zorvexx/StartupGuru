Founder Insights & Tips

💡 1. Validate Pain Points Early

Interview 10 real users before writing code. Make sure they actively want a solution to this problem.

🎯 2. Focus on a Clear Niche

Do not try to build for everyone at once. Solve a specific problem for a targeted group first.

⚡ 3. Keep the MVP Lean

Your minimum viable product should focus on one core feature that delivers clear value immediately.

📊 4. Know Your Unit Economics

Ensure customer acquisition cost (CAC) is lower than lifetime value (LTV) for long-term viability.
